GLOBAL_SUMMARY:

The robot will navigate to the strawberry plants, pick ripe strawberries without damaging the plants, and ensure the strawberries are of good quality before placing them in a container.

None (text-only mode; no frames provided).

The workspace contains rows of strawberry plants with leaves and berries. The robot needs to identify ripe berries based on their color and size, ensuring they are not overly mature or damaged.

- The robot has a camera capable of capturing high-resolution images.
- The robot is equipped with a gripper that can handle delicate objects.
- The robot knows the location of the strawberry plants.
- The robot has access to a container for storing the picked strawberries.

SUCCESS_CRITERIA:

- The robot picks only ripe strawberries.
- The robot does not damage the plants during the picking process.
- The picked strawberries meet the quality standards set by the farm.
- The strawberries are placed in the correct container.

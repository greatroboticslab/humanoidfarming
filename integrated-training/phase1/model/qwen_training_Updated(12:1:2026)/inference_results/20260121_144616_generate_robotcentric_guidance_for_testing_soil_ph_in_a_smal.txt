GLOBAL_SUMMARY:

The robot will navigate to the workspace, collect a soil sample, measure its pH, and provide feedback on the results.

FRAME_BASED_OBSERVATIONS:

None (text-only mode; no frames provided).

INTEGRATED_SCENE_UNDERSTANDING:

The robot needs to identify the soil plot, which is likely marked with a sign or boundary markers. The soil should be accessible without obstacles.

PRECONDITIONS_FOR_ROBOT:

- The robot has a soil sampling tool attached.
- The robot's sensors are calibrated.
- The robot has access to a pH meter.
- The robot knows the location of the soil plot.

SUCCESS_CRITERIA:

- Correctly identified the soil plot.
- Collected a representative soil sample.
- Measured the pH accurately.
- Provided a clear result to the user.
- Stored the data for future reference.

ORDERED_ROBOT_ACTION_STEPS:
1. [type=navigation] Navigate to the marked soil plot.
2. [type=perception] Verify the correct plot by checking the sign.
3. [type=manipulation] Use the soil sampling tool to collect a sample.
4. [type=perception] Measure the pH level using the meter.
5. [type=communication] Announce the measured pH level to the user.
6. [type=manipulation] Return the soil sample to the plot.
7. [type=navigation] Return to the base station.
8. [type=manipulation] Store the data in the robot’s database.
9. [type=communication] Confirm the completion of the task to the user.
10. [type=navigation] Standby and await further instructions.

The robot navigates to the task area and confirms the correct target (ph, small, vegetable, plot) before starting.

It communicates the verified outcome and records the result (Announce the measured pH level to the user.).
Finally, it secures materials and leaves the workspace ready for the next task.

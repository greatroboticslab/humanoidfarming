GLOBAL_SUMMARY:

The robot will navigate to the cucumber plants, identify ripe cucumbers, and harvest them while ensuring they are at the optimal stage of maturity.

FRAME_BASED_OBSERVATIONS:

None (text-only mode; no frames provided).

INTEGRATED_SCENE_UNDERSTANDING:

The workspace contains rows of cucumber plants with various stages of growth. The robot needs to distinguish between immature and mature cucumbers based on their size, color, and texture.

PRECONDITIONS_FOR_ROBOT:

- The robot has access to a database of cucumber characteristics for different stages of maturity.
- The robot is equipped with sensors for measuring cucumber size and color.
- The robot can communicate with a central system for real-time updates on cucumber maturity levels.

SUCCESS_CRITERIA:

- Cucumbers harvested are within the optimal maturity range.
- No damage is done to the plant during the harvesting process.
- The robot accurately identifies and harvests only ripe cucumbers.

ORDERED_ROBOT_ACTION_STEPS:
1. [type=navigation] Move to the nearest row of cucumber plants.
2. [type=perception] Scan the cucumber plants using visual sensors to identify ripe cucumbers.
3. [type=manipulation] Grasp a ripe cucumber using the robotic arm.
4. [type=perception] Verify the cucumber's maturity level by checking its size and color against the database.
5. [type=manipulation] Cut the cucumber from the vine using a specialized tool.
6. [type=manipulation] Place the harvested cucumber into a collection bin.
7. [type=navigation] Return to the starting position.
8. [type=communication] Report the number of harvested cucumbers to the central system.
9. [type=navigation] Move to the next row of cucumber plants.
10. [type=navigation] Return to the base station for maintenance and recharging.

SUBTASK_STORY:
The robot navigates to the task area and positions itself to begin the procedure. It performs the required physical actions using available tools while maintaining safe, controlled motions. It performs a measurement or verification step to ensure the result is reliable before proceeding. Finally, it reports the outcome, records the relevant data, and returns tools to a ready state.

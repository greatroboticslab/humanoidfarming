GLOBAL_SUMMARY:

The robot will plant tomato seeds in seed trays, ensuring proper spacing and depth for optimal growth.

FRAME_BASED_OBSERVATIONS:

None (text-only mode; no frames provided).

INTEGRATED_SCENE_UNDERSTANDING:

The workspace contains seed trays, a packet of tomato seeds, and a small scoop or spoon for handling the soil and seeds. The robot needs to navigate to the correct location, manipulate the soil and seeds, and verify the placement.

PRECONDITIONS_FOR_ROBOT:

- The robot has access to the seed tray and soil.
- The robot can grasp and manipulate the seeds and soil.
- The robot has a sensor for measuring soil moisture and pH levels.

SUCCESS_CRITERIA:

- Seeds are placed at the correct depth.
- Seeds are spaced appropriately within the tray.
- Soil pH is within an acceptable range for tomato growth.
- Proper amount of water is added to the soil.

ORDERED_ROBOT_ACTION_STEPS:
1. [type=navigation] Move to the seed tray location on the workspace.
2. [type=perception] Measure the current soil pH using a built-in sensor.
3. [type=manipulation] Add water to the soil until it reaches the desired pH level, cross-checking with the sensor.
4. [type=manipulation] Scoop a small amount of soil into the palm of the robotic hand.
5. [type=manipulation] Place the soil into the seed tray, creating a small hole for the seed.
6. [type=manipulation] Gently place a tomato seed into the hole, covering it with a small amount of soil.
7. [type=navigation] Move to the next cell in the seed tray and repeat steps 4-6.
8. [type=manipulation] Lightly press down on the soil around the seeds to ensure good contact.
9. [type=navigation] Return/reset to a safe standby position and stow any tools (cleanup).

SUBTASK_STORY:
The robot navigates to the task area and confirms the correct target (tomato, seeds, seed, trays) before starting.
It performs required handling actions to prepare tools and inputs (Add water to the soil until it reaches the desired pH level, cross-checking with the sensor.; Scoop a small amount of soil into the palm of the robotic hand.).
It performs sensing and verification to produce a reliable measurement (Measure the current soil pH using a built-in sensor.).
It communicates the verified outcome and records the result.
Finally, it secures materials and leaves the workspace in a clean state.

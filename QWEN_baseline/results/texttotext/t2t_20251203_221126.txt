Automating greenhouse operations for vegetable production can significantly enhance efficiency, yield, and sustainability. Here’s an overview of how robots can be integrated into these systems:

### 1. **Plant Care and Monitoring**
   - **Pest and Disease Detection:** Robots equipped with cameras and AI algorithms can inspect plants regularly to detect pests or diseases early. This allows for prompt intervention before the problem spreads.
   - **Watering and Fertilization:** Automated watering systems using drones or robots can ensure that plants receive the precise amount of water and nutrients they need, reducing waste and improving plant health.

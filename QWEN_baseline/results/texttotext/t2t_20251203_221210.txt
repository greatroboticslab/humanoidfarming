In humanoid robots, sensors are used to gather information about their environment, which is crucial for tasks such as agriculture, environmental monitoring, and more. When it comes to measuring soil moisture and nutrients, specialized sensors can be integrated into the robot's design or carried along as part of its operational capabilities.

### Soil Moisture Sensors

Soil moisture sensors are typically designed to measure the amount of water present in the soil. There are several types of sensors that can be used:

1. **Capacitive Soil Moisture Sensors**: These sensors use the principle of capacitance change due to changes in soil moisture content. As the soil becomes wetter, the dielectric constant of the soil increases, leading to a change in the sensor's capacitance.

When comparing humanoid robots and wheeled robots for farm logistics and transport, it's important to consider the specific needs of agricultural environments, as well as the capabilities and limitations of each type of robot.

### Humanoid Robots

**Advantages:**
1. **Versatility:** Humanoid robots can perform a wide range of tasks that require human-like dexterity and mobility, such as picking fruits or vegetables, moving heavy loads, or handling fragile items.
2. **Adaptability:** They can adapt to different terrains and environments more easily, including rough fields, uneven ground, and obstacles.
3. **Manipulation Skills:** Humanoid robots have advanced grasping and manipulation capabilities, which can be crucial for tasks like sorting produce or handling delicate items.

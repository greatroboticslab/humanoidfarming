Humanoid robots can play a significant role in supporting sustainable and precision agriculture through various means, enhancing efficiency, accuracy, and environmental sustainability. Here are some ways they can contribute:

1. **Precision Farming**: 
   - **Crop Monitoring**: Robotic systems equipped with cameras and sensors can monitor crop health, detect diseases or pests early, and assess the nutritional status of plants. This information can help farmers make informed decisions about when to apply pesticides, fertilizers, or water.
   - **Soil Analysis**: Robots can collect soil samples and analyze them for nutrient levels, pH, and other critical parameters. This data can be used to tailor fertilization and irrigation practices.

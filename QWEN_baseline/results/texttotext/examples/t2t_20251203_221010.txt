Identifying crop diseases in the field using humanoid robots involves several steps, leveraging various technologies and methodologies to ensure accurate and efficient disease detection. Here’s a general approach:

### 1. Robotic Design and Sensors
- **Robotic Platforms**: The humanoid robot should be designed with mobility to navigate through fields. It should be equipped with sensors for detecting plant health, such as cameras, multispectral imaging devices, thermal cameras, and possibly LIDAR (Light Detection and Ranging) for detailed topographical mapping.

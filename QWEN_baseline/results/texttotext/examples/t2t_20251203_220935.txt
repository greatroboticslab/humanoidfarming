Humanoid robots can play a significant role in assisting farmers during the harvest season by enhancing efficiency, accuracy, and safety on the farm. Here’s how they can be beneficial:

### 1. **Assisting with Manual Tasks**
   - **Picking and Sorting**: Humanoid robots equipped with sensors and cameras can help pick fruits or vegetables from trees or plants. They can be trained to recognize specific types of produce and pick them accurately.
   - **Sorting**: Once picked, these robots can sort the harvested items based on quality (e.g., size, color) and send different batches to different processing stages.

### 2. **Enhancing Precision and Efficiency**
   - **Data Collection**: These robots can collect data on crop health, soil conditions, and weather patterns, which can be crucial

Handling delicate fruits without causing damage is a significant challenge for humanoid robots, as it requires precise manipulation and sensory capabilities that are not yet fully developed in current robotic technologies. However, there are several strategies and technologies that researchers and engineers are exploring to improve this capability:

1. **Soft Robotics**: Soft robotics uses materials like silicone or rubber that can deform and adapt to different shapes, including the contours of fruits. This allows robots to gently grasp and manipulate delicate items without causing harm.

2. **Sensors and Feedback Systems**: Advanced sensors and feedback systems can provide real-time data on the condition of the fruit during handling. This information can be used to adjust the robot's grip pressure and movement speed to ensure the fruit remains intact.

Vertical and indoor farms are innovative agricultural methods that utilize controlled environments to grow crops efficiently and sustainably. Robots play a crucial role in optimizing the operations of these farms, enhancing productivity, reducing labor costs, and improving crop quality. Here’s how robots could operate in vertical and indoor farms:

### 1. **Planting and Transplanting**
- **Seed Planters:** Automated seed planters can be used to plant seeds evenly across the growing trays or beds. These machines can handle different types of seeds and adjust planting depth based on soil conditions.
- **Transplanters:** Once plants reach a certain stage, transplanters can move them from small trays into larger ones or into the final growing beds. This process is essential for maximizing space and ensuring optimal growth conditions.

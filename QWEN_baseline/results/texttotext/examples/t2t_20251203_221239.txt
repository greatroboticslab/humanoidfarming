Humanoid robots have the potential to significantly enhance agricultural productivity and efficiency, particularly in challenging or hazardous environments like farms. Here’s how they can collaborate with human workers:

### 1. **Assisting with Manual Tasks**
   - **Weeding:** Robots equipped with cameras and sensors can identify weeds and selectively remove them using gentle mechanisms that do not harm the crops. This reduces the need for manual weeding, which is both time-consuming and labor-intensive.
   - **Harvesting:** Robotic arms fitted with gripping devices can harvest fruits and vegetables efficiently, reducing the risk of injury to human workers and increasing the speed of the harvesting process.
